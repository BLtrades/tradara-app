## 1. Scope

This policy explains how the operator of Tradara handles personal information
when people use the Tradara website, web application, iPhone app or Android
app. It should be read with the Tradara Terms of Use.

## 2. Information Tradara handles

Tradara handles the information a user provides to create and use an account:

- email address and Supabase account identifier;
- company name, base location, company description and trade preferences;
- preferred project types, travel distance, job-value range and revenue target;
- saved development references, pipeline stage, notes and estimated values; and
- record creation and update times.

The current product does not request precise GPS location, contacts, photos,
camera, microphone, payment-card details, advertising identifiers, health data
or government identifiers. This statement must be rechecked if the product or
its SDKs change.

## 3. Collection and purposes

Information is collected directly from users through account, profile and
pipeline forms. Tradara uses it to authenticate users, recover accounts, sync
records across devices, personalise opportunity rankings, show capacity and
performance insights, provide support and protect the service.

Tradara also requests public South Australian development records from
Location SA / PlanSA. The current request does not include a Tradara account
identifier. Tradara does not sell personal information or use it for
cross-app advertising or tracking in version 1.0.
